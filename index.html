About

Jazz roots, psychedelic edge, atmospheric everything else.

Ted is an LA-based pianist, producer, and recording musician. He started making music at 12 in Virginia — experimental pop first, then jazz piano, then psychedelic rock, abstract electronic, and beat production.

His current work blends jazz harmony, atmospheric electronics, and analog texture into something that doesn't fit a single lane. Herbie Hancock, Radiohead, Boards of Canada, Animal Collective — that general territory.

Available for piano and keys tracking, live performance, touring support, restaurant and private event sets, and production work for the right artists.
